## PART 2 — INTRODUCTION GUIDANCE

**Journal norm, weak signal:** IJPE introductions in the converted sample move from an industry or sustainability transition to a specific supply-chain coordination problem, then to research questions, model preview, concrete contributions, and roadmap.

Apply this structure:

1. Begin with the operational context and managerial stakes of E2W battery swapping.
2. Move quickly from BaaS as a service model to battery ownership as a supply-chain design decision.
3. State the literature gap as an ownership-contract-lifecycle gap, not only as a gap in battery swapping operations.
4. Keep the three research questions explicit.
5. Preview the Stackelberg game and simulation calibration after the research questions.
6. State contributions as concrete advances tied to ownership, lifecycle responsibility, contract coordination, and data-disciplined simulation.
7. Retain a conventional roadmap paragraph.

Do not:

- Add new empirical claims, citations, market statistics, or policy facts.
- Turn the introduction into a broad battery-recycling literature review.
- Use generic phrases such as "this paper contributes to the literature" without specifying the object and mechanism.
- Overstate journal fit as strong evidence; all journal-specific guidance in this MVP is weak-signal.
