## A. Abstract Style

- **Opening move:** Immediately states the modeled supply-chain system.
- **Structure:** System definition -> demand assumptions -> recovery mode -> game structures -> comparison and sensitivity analysis.
- **Contribution placement:** Embedded in technical description.
- **Register:** Model-first and concise.

## B. Introduction Architecture

- **Hook type:** Closed-loop supply-chain practice and recovery benefits.
- **Opening move:** Defines CLSC and contrasts traditional forward flow with reverse flow.
- **Problem-consequence framing:** Uses environmental and economic value of product recovery to motivate the decision problem.
- **Gap identification:** Builds through practical recovery modes and then literature review.
- **Contribution placement:** After practical examples and before the formal model.

- **Literature positioning:** A dedicated "Related literature" section follows, but the introduction also previews the gap.

## C. Contribution Expression

- **Voice:** Problem-question framing rather than an explicit contribution list.

- **Entry point:** Practical collection mechanisms before formal notation.
